# Rent vs Buy Calculator

Make informed housing decisions with our comprehensive calculator based on PWL Capital's "5% Rule" methodology. Compare the true long-term wealth impact of renting versus buying, accounting for opportunity costs, taxes, and market dynamics.

## How It Works

This calculator implements a sophisticated financial model that considers:

- **Property costs**: Taxes, maintenance, PMI, HOA fees
- **Opportunity costs**: Investment returns on down payment and monthly savings
- **Tax implications**: Mortgage interest deduction, SALT limits, capital gains
- **US market factors**: State-specific tax rates, transaction costs
- **Time value**: Net worth projections over 5-30 years

The analysis shows when buying becomes financially advantageous and quantifies the wealth difference between scenarios.

## Key Features

- **Terminal-style interface** for clear data visualization
- **State-specific property tax rates** for accurate calculations  
- **Breakeven analysis** showing crossover points
- **Shareable scenarios** via URL links
- **Export capabilities** for detailed analysis
- **Mobile optimized** for on-the-go decisions
