# Interactive Tools

Calculators and optimization tools to help you make informed financial decisions. All tools work entirely in your browser and don't send data to any servers.

## Available Tools

### [Rent vs Buy Calculator](/tools/rent-vs-buy/)
Compare the long-term wealth impact of renting versus buying a home using PWL Capital's 5% Rule methodology. Features state-specific property taxes, PMI calculations, and comprehensive net worth projections.

### [Retirement Planning Dashboard](/tools/retirement-planning/)
Plan your financial future with Monte Carlo simulations, personalized safe withdrawal rates, and comprehensive tax analysis. Compare multiple retirement scenarios with advanced risk modeling and state-specific tax optimization.

*Additional tools in development:*

- **Credit Card Optimizer**: Find the best credit card combination for your spending patterns
- **Opportunity Cost Calculator**: Quantify the true cost of financial decisions
- **SGOV vs HYSA Comparison**: Compare Treasury bill ETFs to high-yield savings accounts
- **Portfolio Risk Profiler**: Assess appropriate leverage levels for your risk tolerance

## Features

- **Privacy First**: All calculations happen in your browser
- **Share Results**: Generate shareable links with your inputs
- **Mobile Optimized**: Works seamlessly on all devices
- **No Registration**: Use any tool immediately without creating accounts
