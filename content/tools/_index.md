### [> Retirement Planning Calculator](/tools/retirement-calculator/)
Model your path to financial independence with precision. See exactly how compound growth, time, and contributions interact to build wealth over decades.

**What You'll Discover:**
- How small changes in savings rate dramatically impact your timeline
- The true cost of delaying retirement planning by even a few years
- Multiple retirement scenarios compared side-by-side

- Export and share your scenarios

**Features:**
- Multiple scenario comparison (up to 3 retirement ages)

- Shareable URLs for scenarios
