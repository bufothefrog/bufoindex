# Retirement Calculator

A simple calculator to determine the monthly contribution needed to reach your retirement savings goal.

## How It Works

This calculator uses the future value of annuity formula to determine how much you need to save each month to reach your target retirement amount. It accounts for:

- Your current age and desired retirement age
- Your existing savings (which continue to grow)
- Your target retirement amount
- Expected annual investment return

The calculation shows you exactly how much you need to contribute monthly to bridge the gap between where you are now and where you want to be at retirement.
