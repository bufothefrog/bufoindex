# Advanced Techniques

Sophisticated strategies for experienced investors comfortable with higher complexity and risk. These approaches require solid foundational knowledge and careful risk management.

## What You'll Learn

- **Leverage Strategies**: Using borrowed money to amplify returns
- **Portfolio Margin**: Advanced margin techniques for qualified investors
- **Securities-Based Lending**: Using your portfolio as collateral
- **Tax Loss Harvesting**: Advanced tax optimization techniques

**Warning**: These strategies involve significant risk and are not suitable for all investors. Ensure you understand the risks before implementation.
