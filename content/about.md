# About BufoIndex

BufoIndex is a personal finance education platform that bridges the gap between basic financial advice and the sophisticated strategies used by wealthy individuals. Named after Bufo (the founder's frog), it provides transparent documentation of real financial decisions alongside interactive tools for modeling complex scenarios.

## Mission

Our mission is to provide transparent, sophisticated financial education without the usual industry conflicts of interest. No affiliate links, no product sales, no hidden agendas—just educational content focused on wealth optimization strategies.

## Approach

- **Transparency**: Real portfolio decisions documented with percentages
- **Sophistication**: Beyond "save 20%" advice
- **Accessibility**: Complex concepts explained clearly  
- **Practicality**: Actionable strategies you can implement

## Educational Disclaimer

This site provides educational content only and is not personalized financial advice. All strategies involve risk. Please consult qualified professionals before making financial decisions.
