# About BufoIndex

## Our Mission

BufoIndex exists to bridge the gap between basic personal finance advice and sophisticated investment strategies. Most financial education stops at "max out your 401(k)" and "emergency fund," leaving a vast middle ground unexplored. We focus on that space—providing practical strategies for people ready to move beyond the basics but not quite ready for institutional-level complexity.

## Why "Bufo"?

The name comes from *Bufo*, the scientific genus for true toads. Like these amphibians that bridge aquatic and terrestrial environments, BufoIndex helps bridge the gap between simple and sophisticated financial strategies. Plus, frogs are known for their ability to see opportunities others miss—a fitting metaphor for spotting overlooked financial optimizations.

## Author Background

Content is created by someone with:

- 15+ years in quantitative finance and risk management
- Experience across institutional asset management (60%), fintech (25%), and consulting (15%)
- Focus on systematic strategies and risk-adjusted returns
- No financial advisory credentials by choice—this keeps content educational rather than prescriptive

## Important Disclaimers

### Educational Purpose Only

All content on BufoIndex is for educational purposes only. Nothing here constitutes:

- Personal financial advice
- Investment recommendations
- Tax or legal guidance
- A guarantee of future results

### Do Your Own Research

Financial strategies that work for one person may not work for another. Before implementing any strategy:

- Consult qualified professionals (CFP, CPA, attorney)
- Consider your personal risk tolerance and financial situation
- Understand the risks and potential downsides
- Start small and scale gradually

### Risk Warning

Many strategies discussed involve significant risk, including:

- **Leverage strategies**: Can amplify losses as well as gains
- **Market risk**: All investments can lose value
- **Concentration risk**: Focused strategies increase volatility
- **Liquidity risk**: Some strategies limit access to your money

**Past performance does not guarantee future results.**
